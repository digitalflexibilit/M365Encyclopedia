### Copilot for legal
- **Status:** GA
- **Last change:** 2026-10, more than 20 legal skills
- **More info:** [Manage Copilot for legal](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/admin/manage/manage-copilot-for-legal?view=o365-worldwide)

Legal skills in Copilot in Word help legal professionals draft, redline and review contracts. The plugin is on by default in Word desktop for commercial Copilot subscribers, and admins control it with the policy Legal Skills in M365 Copilot in Word.
