# Word

Last updated: 2026-11-16

## Current features

### Copilot for legal
- **Status:** GA
- **Last change:** 2026-10, more than 20 legal skills
- **More info:** [Manage Copilot for legal](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/admin/manage/manage-copilot-for-legal?view=o365-worldwide)

Legal skills in Copilot in Word help legal professionals draft, redline and review contracts. The plugin is on by default in Word desktop for commercial Copilot subscribers, and admins control it with the policy Legal Skills in M365 Copilot in Word.

### Heading checks in Accessibility Assistant
- **Status:** Preview
- **Last change:** 2026-11-09, announced to Microsoft 365 Insiders
- **More info:** [Improved detection for improper or missing headings in Word](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft365insiderblog/improved-detection-for-improper-or-missing-headings-in-word/4506225)

Accessibility Assistant in Word for Windows flags text that looks like a heading but has no heading style, sections of three or more paragraphs without a heading after a page or section break, and headings split across several paragraphs. The checks are under Check Accessibility, Document Structure. Correct headings help screen reader users, the Navigation Pane, tables of contents and Copilot. Needs Version 2610 (Build 20620.20002) or later.
