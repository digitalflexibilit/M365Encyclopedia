# OneNote

Last updated: 2026-10-21

## Current features

### Copilot Notebooks in OneNote
- **Status:** GA
- **Last change:** 2026-07, available in OneNote on the web, iPhone and iPad
- **More info:** [Microsoft 365 Copilot release notes](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/release-notes)

Copilot Notebooks bring notes, Office files and Copilot chats into one workspace that Copilot uses as context. End users can create them in OneNote on Windows, the web, iPhone and iPad, and OneNote on iPhone can capture audio, images and typed notes in one session. A Microsoft 365 Copilot license is needed. The lighter Notebooks design in the Microsoft 365 Copilot app (September 2026) sits next to the fuller OneNote experience.
