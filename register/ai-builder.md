### End of AI Builder credits
- **Status:** Retiring
- **Last change:** 2026-11-01, end of life for capacity add-ons and seeded credits

AI Builder credits are being phased out. From 1 November 2026 add-ons reach end of life and new premium licenses carry no seeded credits. AI Builder features keep working and use Copilot Credits.
