# Loop

Last updated: 2026-11-11

## Current features

### Meeting notes stored in the organizer's OneDrive
- **Status:** Rolling out
- **Last change:** 2026-11-11, roadmap status changed to rolling out, November 2026
- **More info:** [Microsoft 365 Roadmap item 576194](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=576194)

For Teams meetings outside channels, collaborative meeting notes, including Loop-powered notes and Facilitator notes, are stored in the meeting organizer's OneDrive instead of the OneDrive of the person who starts the notes. This gives more consistent ownership and management of meeting content. The note-taking experience for participants does not change. Applies to Teams on desktop, web, Mac, iOS and Android, in worldwide and GCC clouds.
