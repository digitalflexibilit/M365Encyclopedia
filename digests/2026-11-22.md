# Digest 2026-11-22

## Copilot
- **Claude Opus 5.5 Fast option in Cowork** (Copilot Cowork, GA): Claude Opus 5.5 in the Cowork model selector now has a Fast option for quicker responses. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/cowork/whats-new)
- **GPT 5.5 model in Cowork** (Copilot Cowork, Retired): GPT 5.5 is no longer in the Cowork model selector. GPT 6.1 Sol, hosted by Microsoft in Azure, replaces it. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/cowork/whats-new)

## Teams
- **Agentic Users** (Teams, Preview): Agents get their own Entra account and join teams under their own name, with an accountable owner. People can add them to chats, channels and meetings and @mention them, and admins approve, monitor and disable them through Agent 365. [More info](https://devblogs.microsoft.com/microsoft365dev/new-capabilities-for-building-digital-teammates-in-microsoft-teams/)
- **Threaded replies, plans and HTML widgets for agents** (Teams, Preview): Agent chats get a thread per task, visible plans and progress for multistep work, and interactive HTML widgets in responses. Socket mode lets developers test agents without a public endpoint. [More info](https://devblogs.microsoft.com/microsoft365dev/new-capabilities-for-building-digital-teammates-in-microsoft-teams/)
- **Synthetic audio and video detection in meetings** (Teams, Preview): Admins can choose a certified third-party deepfake detection provider in a meeting policy. Its bot analyzes scheduled and recurring meetings, and the organizer can remove a flagged participant. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/third-party-synthetic-audio-video-detection-meetings)
- **Explicit mode for Information Barriers in Teams** (Teams, Preview): Admins can convert a team from implicit to explicit Information Barriers mode. [More info](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/spblog/microsoft-365-copilot-and-ai-agent-readiness-and-resiliency-with-sharepoint-cont/4573034)

## SharePoint
- **Update: Content Governance Hub** (SharePoint, Roadmap): Reported yesterday without a date. The hub in the SharePoint admin center is now expected to reach GA in December 2026, and a separate Governance Hub for site owners is in public preview. [More info](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/spblog/microsoft-365-copilot-and-ai-agent-readiness-and-resiliency-with-sharepoint-cont/4573034)
- **Application Restrictions for apps and agents** (SharePoint, Rolling out): Admins can use PowerShell to set per-site allow or deny lists of third-party apps and agent blueprints, plus a tenant-wide deny list, even for apps that already have permissions. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sharepoint/restrict-application-agent-access)
- **File archive policies** (SharePoint, Preview): Admins can archive individual inactive files in SharePoint and OneDrive by last access date and file type, with a what-if mode first. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/powershell/module/microsoft.online.sharepoint.powershell/new-spofilearchivepolicy?view=sharepoint-ps)
- **Tenant-wide version trimming** (SharePoint, Preview): Admins can trim old file versions across the tenant with a what-if analysis, through PowerShell first. [More info](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/spblog/microsoft-365-copilot-and-ai-agent-readiness-and-resiliency-with-sharepoint-cont/4573034)
- **SharePoint extra storage pay-as-you-go** (SharePoint, Preview): A pay-as-you-go meter for storage above quota, with GA planned for January 2027 at USD 0.20 per GB per month. [More info](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/spblog/microsoft-365-copilot-and-ai-agent-readiness-and-resiliency-with-sharepoint-cont/4573034)
- **Multi-Geo default geo move** (SharePoint, Preview): Multi-Geo customers can move their default geo to a more specific region, such as Germany or Korea. [More info](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/spblog/microsoft-365-copilot-and-ai-agent-readiness-and-resiliency-with-sharepoint-cont/4573034)
- **Agent access heatmap and App Intelligence** (SharePoint, Roadmap): Both are expected in December 2026. The heatmap shows agent access by sensitivity, department and platform, and App Intelligence helps admins review and restrict third-party apps with high-privilege access. [More info](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/spblog/microsoft-365-copilot-and-ai-agent-readiness-and-resiliency-with-sharepoint-cont/4573034)
- **Restrict collaboration by agentic users** (SharePoint, Roadmap): A setting that limits Agentic Users to sharing content only with their manager, expected in public preview in December 2026. [More info](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/spblog/microsoft-365-copilot-and-ai-agent-readiness-and-resiliency-with-sharepoint-cont/4573034)

## OneDrive
- **Update: Archiving and sync controls** (OneDrive, Roadmap): The file archive policies announced yesterday are now in public preview through PowerShell, including for OneDrive accounts. Sync controls still have no date. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/powershell/module/microsoft.online.sharepoint.powershell/new-spofilearchivepolicy?view=sharepoint-ps)

## Admin & governance
- **Update: Copilot Credits cost management** (Agent 365, Preview): Now expanded as Agent 365 Cost Management in preview, with spending limits down to resource level, controls on which models teams can use, budgets delegated to business leaders, and Fabric export for chargeback. [More info](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/agent-365-blog/whats-new-in-microsoft-agent-365-ignite-2026/4571753)
- **Microsoft 365 Backup recovery window up to 10 years** (Microsoft 365 Backup, GA): Admins set the recovery window per policy, from 3 months up to 10 years for SharePoint and OneDrive and up to 2 years for Exchange. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/backup/backup-view-edit-policies?view=o365-worldwide#configure-the-backup-recovery-window)
- **Teams channel backup** (Microsoft 365 Backup, Preview): A private preview protects channels with their SharePoint content and restores deleted or edited channel messages within 30 days. [More info](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/spblog/microsoft-365-copilot-and-ai-agent-readiness-and-resiliency-with-sharepoint-cont/4573034)
- **Agent Rewind** (Microsoft 365 Backup, Preview): A private preview lets admins roll Microsoft 365 resources back to a chosen point in time, with anomaly detection and prioritized recovery. [More info](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/spblog/microsoft-365-copilot-and-ai-agent-readiness-and-resiliency-with-sharepoint-cont/4573034)
