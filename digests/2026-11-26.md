# Digest 2026-11-26

## Copilot
- **Copilot Chat in shared and delegate mailboxes** (Outlook, Rolling out): In classic Outlook, users with full access or folder-level permission on a shared or delegate mailbox that is added as a resource can open Copilot Chat directly from that mailbox. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=573698)
- **Copilot in read-only workbooks** (Excel, Rolling out): Copilot chat now answers questions about workbooks opened as read-only, such as what the data shows, in Excel for Windows, Mac and web. [More info](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/excelblog/whats-new-in-excel-november-2026/4573867)
- **Descriptive formula error cards** (Excel, Rolling out): Hovering over a formula error in Excel for Windows (version 2611 or later) explains the error, its likely causes and how to fix it, and for supported errors a "Fix this error" button asks Copilot to repair the formula. [More info](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/excelblog/whats-new-in-excel-november-2026/4573867)
- **Accounting skills in Copilot in Excel** (Excel, Preview): Built-in skills for reconciliation, journal entries, period-end close, financial statements and variance analysis are rolling out to the Frontier program in Excel and Cowork. [More info](https://support.microsoft.com/excel/copilot/copilot-in-excel-built-in-skills)
- **Edit with Copilot in Excel on iPhone** (Excel, GA): Users can edit workbooks on iPhone with natural language, and Copilot keeps working in the cloud after they leave the app and notifies them when the task is done. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=577177)
- **Update: Business Apps in Work IQ** (Microsoft 365 Copilot, Preview): Power Apps model-driven apps are now available as Business Apps in Work IQ, through the Work IQ tool in Copilot Studio or Work IQ MCP chat. Users can list, read, create, update and delete records in natural language, and changes need explicit confirmation. The roadmap item is marked launched. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=571888)
- **Update: Copilot Pages** (Microsoft 365 Copilot, Roadmap): A new roadmap item lets users open a page shared in a Teams chat or channel directly in Teams, in the main window or a new window, to edit and discuss it. Planned for January 2027. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=580886)
- **Access management for Copilot Analytics** (Viva Insights, Roadmap): Global, AI and Viva Insights admins will manage who can use Copilot Analytics in the product itself, with access policies that pair a permission set with a partition, instead of using Viva Feature Access Management and Manager settings. Planned for January 2027. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=578862)

## Teams
- **Expanded Markdown support** (Teams, Roadmap): The compose box will accept more Markdown syntax to structure and format messages. Planned for January 2027. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=576199)
- **Onboarding workflow for new team members** (Teams, Roadmap): New members of a team are automatically added to the relevant channels when they join. In development. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=573452)
- **Add tag members to channels automatically** (Teams, Roadmap): Channels can be linked to a tag, so people added to the tag automatically become members of those channels. In development. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=577174)
- **Shared content on both room displays** (Teams, Roadmap): On Teams Rooms on Android with two displays, content shared over HDMI outside a meeting can show on both screens, controlled from the room console and admin settings. Planned for January 2027. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=582052)

## Outlook
- **Manage multiple inbox rules at once** (Outlook, Rolling out): In new Outlook for Windows and Outlook on the web, users can select several or all inbox rules and delete, enable or disable them in one action. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=569430)

## OneDrive
- **OneDrive pay-as-you-go storage** (OneDrive, Rolling out): A consumption meter lets admins allow selected OneDrive accounts to grow beyond their licensed storage, billed at USD 0.20 per GB per month for the usage above the licensed quota. Requires pay-as-you-go billing with an Azure subscription; not available for education, GCC or 21Vianet. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/commerce/add-storage-space#add-microsoft-365-onedrive-storage)

## Dataverse
- **Update: Filter-based security** (Dataverse, Roadmap): Admins and makers will be able to create and manage filter-based security filters in a simplified experience in the Power Platform maker portal. Planned for March 2027. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=581895)
