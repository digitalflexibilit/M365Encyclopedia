# Digest 2026-11-25

## Copilot
- **Usage-based billing models in Chat and Office** (Microsoft 365 Copilot, Roadmap): Licensed users who reach a model usage limit in Copilot Chat or Word, Excel and PowerPoint can switch to Auto at no extra cost, or keep their chosen model and pay with Copilot Credits if usage-based billing is on. A credits icon and the /cost prompt show metered use, and admins allow it through a new spending policy service. Frontier in early December, general availability in mid-December 2026. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/plan/usage-based-billing-overview-copilot-credits)
- **Update: Copilot Workspaces** (Microsoft 365 Copilot, Roadmap): New roadmap items add workspace suggestions, a catch-up view of what changed and what is open, and workspace memory for details, preferences and recurring instructions. Preview in November, general availability in December 2026. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=579889)
- **Update: Copilot Pages** (Microsoft 365 Copilot, Roadmap): Users will be able to comment on a page to ask Copilot questions or request specific edits, with people and Copilot editing together. Preview in November, general availability in December 2026. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=580449)
- **Update: Learning agent** (Microsoft 365 Copilot, Roadmap): Learning agent will send personalized AI and Copilot upskilling notifications in Teams based on role, adoption stage and usage. Preview in November, general availability in December 2026. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=579905)
- **Service Agent in Microsoft 365** (Microsoft 365 Copilot, Roadmap): A customer service agent grounded in company knowledge, Microsoft 365 data and optional CRM connections, with semi or fully autonomous mode and activity monitoring for managers. Preview planned for December 2026. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=577829)
- **Work IQ real-time transcription for meetings and calls** (Work IQ, Roadmap): Authorized apps and agents can read a Teams meeting or call transcript while it happens through Work IQ and MCP. It needs permissions and consent and does not start transcription. Preview in December 2026, general availability in January 2027. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=572674)
- **Code node in workflows** (Copilot Studio, Roadmap): Makers can run Python as a workflow step, written by hand or generated from a description, in a sandbox that consumes Copilot Credits. General availability on December 1, 2026. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=580866)
- **Update: Agent node in workflows** (Copilot Studio, Roadmap): The agent node can return files it creates (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, CSV) as workflow outputs for later steps. General availability on December 3, 2026. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/microsoft-copilot-studio/workflows-experience/agent-node-workflow)
- **Workflow co-owners** (Copilot Studio, Roadmap): Makers can share a workflow with co-owners who can edit, manage and run it, after reviewing its connections. Planned for December 2026. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=580864)
- **Service principal owner for workflows** (Copilot Studio, Roadmap): Workflows can be owned by a Microsoft Entra app registration instead of a named user. Planned for December 2026. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=580863)

## Teams
- **Voice phishing detection for external calls** (Teams, Roadmap): AI analyzes one-to-one calls with external people for signs of social engineering and alerts the user. Preview in December 2026, general availability in January 2027. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=580443)
- **Allowed external meeting bots** (Teams, Roadmap): Admins can list the external meeting bots, such as notetakers, allowed to join meetings and block or lobby all others. A new admin center report will show detected bots and their meeting access. Planned for January 2027. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=580451)
- **View files and conversations side by side** (Teams, Roadmap): Opening a PDF, Markdown or HTML file from a message shows the conversation next to the file so users can reply without closing it. General availability from late December 2026 to early January 2027. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=574094)
- **Event registrations up to 20,000** (Teams, Roadmap): Town hall and webinar registrations scale with attendee capacity, up to 3,000 with Teams Enterprise and 20,000 with Attendee Capacity Packs. Timeline updated: preview in December 2026, general availability in January 2027. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=574110)
- **Raise hand and reactions in events with up to 100,000 attendees** (Teams, Roadmap): Attendees at the largest events can send reactions and raise their hand, and organizers can let them speak on camera. Planned for December 2026. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=581720)
- **Facilitator in Teams mobile with speaker attribution** (Teams, Roadmap): Facilitator on a phone takes notes of in-person conversations and shows who said what, with a summary and follow-ups. Planned for December 2026. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=579864)
- **Open channels at last read message** (Teams, Roadmap): Teams desktop users can choose to open channels at their last read message. Planned for February 2027. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=579890)

## Outlook

- **Custom flag descriptions** (Outlook, Roadmap): Users can add their own text to a follow-up flag in new Outlook for Windows. Planned for February 2027. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=580885)

## PowerPoint

## SharePoint
- **Classic publishing sites and classic pages** (SharePoint, Retiring): From March 1, 2027 no tenant can create classic publishing sites or turn on publishing features. Classic wiki, Web Part, blog, publishing and custom ASPX pages become read-only in all tenants from October 1, 2028. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/sharepoint/classic-user-created-page-deprecation)
- **Organization-wide page templates** (SharePoint, Roadmap): Approved page templates, managed in the brand center site, are available to authors on every site. Planned for December 2026. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=581865)
- **Refreshed Lists experience** (SharePoint, Roadmap): Lists get a reorganized command bar, folder breadcrumbs, more visible views and filter pills. Planned for December 2026. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=578879)
