# Digest 2026-11-02

## Power Apps
- **AI-generated descriptions for model-driven apps** (Power Apps, Retiring): After 30 November 2026 the "Create descriptions using AI" option is removed from the Publish dialog and app designer. Existing descriptions are not affected. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/important-changes-coming)
- **Power Apps Ideas and formula generation in canvas apps** (Power Apps, Retiring): After 11 December 2026, Power Apps Ideas, "Create a formula" and formula generation from code comments are removed. "Explain this formula" stays. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/important-changes-coming)
- **Cross-environment comment import and export** (Power Apps, Retiring): After 31 December 2026 the dedicated action to move comments between environments in component designers is removed. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/important-changes-coming)

## Dataverse
- **Power Platform dataflows in Dataverse for Teams** (Dataverse, Retiring): New dataflows can no longer be created. Refresh of existing dataflows stops on 1 December 2026 and entry points are removed from 1 January 2027. Dataflows elsewhere are not affected. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-query/dataflows/dataflows-teams)
