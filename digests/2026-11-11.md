# Digest 2026-11-11

## Copilot
- **Claude models in the EU Data Boundary** (Microsoft 365 Copilot, Roadmap): Microsoft announced on 5 November that Claude models in Copilot, including Copilot Cowork, will be processed inside the EU Data Boundary for EU and EFTA organizations by the end of 2026. Processing moves automatically, and the Claude admin setting stays off by default in the EU and EFTA. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/blog/2026/11/05/bringing-claude-models-in-microsoft-copilot-into-the-eu-data-boundary/)
- **Copilot Chat fair use limits for unlicensed users** (Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat, Rolling out): Users without a Microsoft 365 Copilot license get fair use limits on more Copilot Chat features. Rollout started on 2 November for organizations with 2,000 or fewer seats and on 9 November for larger ones. Admins cannot view or change the limits. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/announcements/2026-november)

## Teams
- **Require consent before joining meetings** (Teams, Rolling out): A new Teams admin center setting lets admins require explicit consent from every participant before they join a meeting hosted in the organization. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/officeupdates/teams-admin)
- **Profanity filtering control for meeting transcripts** (Teams, Rolling out): Admins can use a meeting policy to turn masking of profane words in live transcription and saved transcripts on or off. It is on by default, and live captions keep following each participant's own setting. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/officeupdates/teams-admin)
- **Keep calls visible in a separate window on the web** (Teams, Rolling out): In Teams for the web and the PWA, a call can stay in a simplified window on top of other content when the user leaves the browser tab. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/officeupdates/teams-admin)

## Loop
- **Meeting notes stored in the organizer's OneDrive** (Loop, Rolling out): For Teams meetings outside channels, Loop meeting notes and Facilitator notes are now stored in the organizer's OneDrive instead of the OneDrive of the person who starts the notes. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=576194)

## Power Apps
- **Attachments modern control in canvas apps** (Power Apps, Preview): A modern Attachments control lets users add, download and remove files on a record, also outside a form, with limits for file count, size and type. Its output can go straight to Patch for SharePoint or Dataverse. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-apps/maker/canvas-apps/controls/modern-controls/modern-control-attachments)
- **Copresence in Power Apps plans and record-level copresence in model-driven apps** (Power Apps, Retiring): After 30 November 2026 plans and model-driven app records no longer show who else is working on them. Record sharing is not affected. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/important-changes-coming)
- **Rename controls with Copilot** (Power Apps, Retiring): After 11 December 2026 Copilot no longer suggests names for canvas app controls. Makers rename controls in Tree view. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/important-changes-coming)

## Power Automate
- **Describe it to design it** (Power Automate, Retiring): From 30 November 2026 makers can no longer create cloud flows by describing them in plain language. Existing flows are not affected, and Microsoft points to the Power Automate plugin in an approved AI assistant instead. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/important-changes-coming)
