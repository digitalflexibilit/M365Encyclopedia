# Digest 2026-11-17

## Copilot
- **Copilot Workspaces** (Microsoft 365 Copilot, Rolling out): Copilot Notebooks is renamed Copilot Workspaces, a shared place where a team works on files and chats together with Copilot, with presence and invitations. Existing notebooks convert automatically with their content and sharing, and Workspaces will reach OneNote over time. [More info](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/copilot-notebooks-to-copilot-workspaces)

## Teams
- **Malicious QR code detection in messages** (Teams, Rolling out): Malicious URL protection now checks URLs embedded in QR codes shared in chats, channels and meeting messages, and warns the sender and recipients when a destination looks malicious. It is part of baseline protection and needs no extra license. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/microsoftteams/malicious-url-protection-teams)
