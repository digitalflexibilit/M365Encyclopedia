# Digest 2026-10-23

## Copilot
- **Copilot in SharePoint: PDFs, version history and Advanced Autofill** (SharePoint, Rolling out): Copilot can work with PDFs at page and section level (extract sections, reorder pages, split files), read version history, explain changes and restore an earlier version, and autofill library metadata from a description. General availability of Copilot in SharePoint began rolling out on 30 September 2026. [More info](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/spblog/what%E2%80%99s-new-in-copilot-in-sharepoint-october-2026/4535423)
- **Expanded email triage with Copilot** (Outlook, Rolling out): Copilot can delete, move, copy and categorize messages, create folders and help create inbox rules, in Copilot Chat and in Outlook on Windows, web, Mac and mobile. Actions on more than five emails need confirmation. English only. [More info](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook/triage-email-with-microsoft-365-copilot-in-outlook)
- **Notebooks: Markdown, TXT and RTF sources** (Microsoft 365 Copilot, Rolling out): Notebooks accept Markdown, TXT and RTF files as sources and have a refreshed navigation pane. [More info](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft-copilot-blog/whats-new-in-notebooks--october-2026/4559105)

## Power Platform
- **Canvas authoring agent plugin** (Power Apps, GA): People and coding agents such as GitHub Copilot or Claude Code can coauthor the same canvas app through an MCP server, creating screens, adding controls and writing Power Fx. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/blog/power-apps/turbocharge-your-canvas-development-with-the-canvas-authoring-agent-plugin-now-generally-available/)
- **Release plans replaced by the AI at Work roadmap** (Power Platform, Update): Release plans are no longer published from September 2026. New Dynamics 365, Power Platform and Dataverse capabilities appear in the AI at Work roadmap. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/release-plan/2026wave1/microsoft-copilot-studio/planned-features)
