# Digest 2026-11-28

## Copilot
- **Cowork Plugins page** (Copilot Cowork, GA): The Customize page in Copilot Cowork is renamed to Plugins. It still hosts plugins and skills in one place, where users browse, install, share and create them. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/cowork/cowork-customize)
- **Update: Cowork models** (Copilot Cowork, Retired): Besides GPT 5.5 (reported on 22 November), GPT 5.6 Terra is also no longer available in the Cowork model selector. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/cowork/whats-new)

## Teams
- **File upload and download authentication for agents** (Teams, Rolling out): Developers of Teams agents that send or receive files must update their code and declare new resource-specific consent permissions in the app manifest (available from 15 January 2027). File operations stop working for agents not updated by 1 March 2027. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/platform/bots/how-to/bots-filesv4)

## SharePoint
- **App-only permission for tenant App Catalog writes** (SharePoint, Rolling out): From 31 January 2027, app-only apps that add or update packages or Tenant-Wide Extensions in the tenant App Catalog need the SharePointTenantSettings.ReadWrite.All permission on the SharePoint resource; Sites.ReadWrite.All alone will no longer be enough. UI, delegated, read and delete operations are not affected. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sharepoint/dev/spfx/tenant-app-catalog-app-only-permissions)

## Other Microsoft 365 apps
- **Viva Glint multi-attribute report export enhancements** (Viva Glint, Roadmap): The multi-attribute report export will be available to more users, support larger exports and let authorized users choose the questions and attributes to include. Planned for January 2027. [More info](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?filters=&searchterms=580455)
