# Digest 2026-10-30

## Teams
- **Agent response streaming in all conversation types** (Teams, GA): Agents can now stream replies in group chats, channels and meeting chats, not only one-on-one chats. Developers need the latest Teams SDK, or must set `replyToId` in the REST API. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/platform/developer-announcements)
