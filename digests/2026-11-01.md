# Digest 2026-11-01

## Power BI
- **Power BI Q&A retirement date moved** (Power BI, Retiring): Update: Learn pages now say Q&A experiences are going away in February 2027. Earlier documentation named December 2026. Q&A is the legacy natural language feature on dashboards and in Q&A visuals. Microsoft recommends Copilot for Power BI as the replacement. [More info](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/natural-language/q-and-a-intro)
